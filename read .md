# AI Resume Analyzer

An AI-powered web application that analyzes resumes against job descriptions, identifies missing skills, and provides actionable recommendations to help candidates improve their resumes and increase their job-match score.

## 🚀 Project Overview

The AI Resume Analyzer helps job seekers understand how well their resumes match a particular job role. Users can upload their resumes, enter a job description, and receive an analysis of their skills, qualifications, experience, and areas for improvement.

The system uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to extract relevant information from resumes and compare it with job requirements.

## ✨ Features

- **Resume Upload:** Upload resumes in PDF or DOCX format.
- **Resume Parsing:** Extract relevant information such as skills, education, work experience, and qualifications.
- **Job Description Analysis:** Analyze job descriptions to identify required skills and qualifications.
- **Job Match Score:** Calculate a resume-match score based on the relationship between resume content and job requirements.
- **Missing Skills Detection:** Identify important skills mentioned in the job description but missing from the resume.
- **Resume Improvement Suggestions:** Provide recommendations to improve resume content and relevance.
- **Skill Match Visualization:** Display matching and missing skills through charts and visual indicators.
- **Analysis History:** Save and review previous resume analyses.
- **User Authentication:** Allow users to securely register, log in, and access their analysis history.
